Support

I lost my phone. Can I get my account back?

If you registered with a security key or a passkey that syncs through iCloud, sign in on your new phone with that key — your identity lives in the public directory and your friendships re-verify. Your old message history does not transfer: it was encrypted for your old device, and by design nobody — including us — can recover it.

Why do I have to meet someone in person to add them?

Because that's the protection. Anyone on the internet can fake a name, a number, or a voice. Nobody can fake being physically next to you. The in-person setup is the reason a scammer can never appear inside your Seal.

Someone asked me for money in Seal. Is it automatically safe?

Sealed means it truly came from that person's phone — it does not mean the request is wise, or that their phone couldn't be in someone else's hands. If a request feels off, call them. Seal makes sure you know who you're talking to; the judgment is still yours.

My messages aren't arriving.

Both phones need the current version of Seal and an internet connection. Messages send automatically once you're back online — a small clock icon means a message is waiting to go. If a chat shows a verification notice, open it and follow the plain-language instructions; if it persists, have both people open Seal once and try again.

How do I report abuse?

Long-press any message from another person and choose Report. A pre-filled email opens with the flagged message attached; sending it also blocks that person for you immediately. We review every report and act within 24 hours, up to permanently disabling the reported identity.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete identity. This permanently removes you from the directory and wipes the app. It cannot be undone.

Is Seal on Android?

Not yet — Seal is currently iPhone-only, by invite. Android is on the roadmap. If your family needs it, tell us; it helps us decide how fast.
