02 / Why a key

Nobody opens it early. Not even us.

Your envelopes open only if you stop opening Seal for a long time, you get weeks of warnings, and enough of the people holding keys agree. If you open the app once, everything stops.

A key is something a person can hold in a drawer for years. When the day comes, they tap it on any iPhone, or on an Android phone or computer at sealmessenger.com/open. They do not need an iPhone of their own.

03 / Start

From the box to sealed.

  1. Get the app. Install Seal on your iPhone and sign up with Face ID. It takes a minute.
  2. Write. One envelope per person. A letter, photos, a voice message, and the passwords. Seal can ask you questions if you do not know where to start.
  3. Set up the keys. For each person, type their name and tap their key on your phone twice. That key is now theirs.
  4. Seal it. Tap Seal. Then give each person their key and their printed page, by hand or by mail.

04 / How many

One key per person you trust.

Three is a common choice. Then any two of them together can open your envelopes, and one person alone cannot.

Your own sign-in uses Face ID, so you do not need a key for yourself. A spare key for your own backup is a good idea, but it is not required.

The setup Seal is built around: a wife, a brother and an attorney each hold a key. Any two of them can open the envelopes, after 90 days of silence and three weeks of warnings.
