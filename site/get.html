Choose a kit Who needs a key?

01 / One per person

Not one key per letter.

A physical key belongs to a person. Buy one for a recipient, a key holder, or as your own backup. That person can use the same Seal identity across the envelopes and roles connected to them.

Security keys are optional for most Seal users. A passkey with Face ID or Touch ID also works. Buy a physical key when you want a separate object that can be handed over and kept for years.

02 / In every kit

1

Compatible key

The exact model listed on the kit. No guessing among similar-looking security keys.

2

Printed setup card

The short handoff steps, what the key is for, and where to open envelopes later.

3

Human support

Email a person if setup is confusing. The margin also supports continued work on Seal.

04 / The honest version

You do not have to buy it here.

You can buy a compatible key directly from its maker. A Seal kit costs more because it arrives as one understandable package with printed instructions, fulfillment and support.

Do not buy a key for every envelope. Buy one only for each person who needs a physical key.

Before ordering

Compatibility Seal checks a key during setup and refuses it before saving anything if the required feature is missing.

Shipping US addresses only at first. Final shipping price and dispatch time will appear before checkout opens.

Returns The return policy will be stated before orders open. A broken arrival will be replaced.

Privacy Payment details will be handled by Stripe. Order information is used only for fulfillment and support.
