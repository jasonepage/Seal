02 / Why a key

Nobody can peek. Not even us.

The box stays shut while you are around. If you stop using Seal for a long time, your phone warns you every day for weeks. Only then can the people with keys use them together and open it.

03 / How it works

Four easy steps.

  1. Get the app. It is free. You sign in with your face.
  2. Write. A letter for each person, plus any passwords they will need. Stuck? The app asks you questions to help.
  3. Set up the keys. Plug each key into your phone. Now that key belongs to that person.
  4. Lock it. Tap Seal. Then hand out the keys and the printed pages.

04 / How many keys

One key for each person you trust.

Three is a good number. Then any two of them can open the box together. One person alone cannot.

You do not need a key for yourself. Your face is your key.

For example: your wife, your brother and your lawyer each get a key. If something happens to you, any two of them can open it.
