Questions

What if they lose the key?

Set up a new key for them in Seal, then seal the letter again.

Can Seal read my letter?

No. Your letter is locked on your phone before it leaves. Only the key you chose can open it.

Do they need an iPhone?

No. A key opens letters on any phone or computer at sealmessenger.com/open. Writing a letter needs an iPhone with iOS 18 or newer.

What does it cost?

The app is free, and letters that open right away are free to seal. One payment of $29.99 in the app unlocks more ways to seal, for good. No subscription. Kits are $49 or $119, plus $7.95 shipping per order.

Where do keys come from?

Buy a kit here, or use a key you already own that can hold a secret, like a current YubiKey 5.
