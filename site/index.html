How Seal works

1 · Set up in person

You connect with your family face to face — a quick tap between phones when you're together. That moment creates a bond a scammer on the internet can never copy. No passwords, no phone numbers, nothing to steal remotely.

2 · Message privately

Everything in Seal is end-to-end encrypted and signed by the phone that sent it. If a message shows up in Seal, it really came from your person — not someone spoofing their number.

3 · Seal what matters

Money requests, account details, important instructions travel as Sealed Cards — locked exactly as written, impossible to alter, clearly marked. Anything that arrives anywhere else? Hang up. It's not them.

Why not just text?

Phone numbers get spoofed. SIMs get swapped. Voices get cloned from ten seconds of audio. Every channel your family uses today trusts a number or a voice — the two things scammers fake best. Seal trusts neither: identity lives in the phone's secure chip, established in person. And honestly? Seal proves a message came from your person's phone. It doesn't judge whether a request is wise — that's still a family conversation. We just make sure you're having it with the real person.

Built different, on purpose

No servers to breach — Seal has no backend collecting your messages. No accounts to hack — there are no passwords or resets. No data to sell — we can't read anything, and we wouldn't want to. Currently on iPhone via invite. Android is on the roadmap.
