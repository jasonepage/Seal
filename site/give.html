Why does the Lightning kit cost more?

The Lightning key has two plugs, Lightning and USB-C, so it fits every iPhone and most computers. That key costs much more to buy. The rest of the kit is the same.

Before you give it

Plain facts.

Ask first

Seal works best when they want it. It does not let you see their phone or read what they write.

Do they need an iPhone?

No. A key opens letters on any phone or computer at sealmessenger.com/open. Writing a letter needs an iPhone with iOS 18 or newer.

Which kit fits?

Look at the end of their charging cable. A small oval plug is USB-C, for iPhone 15 or newer. A thin flat plug is Lightning, for iPhone 14 or earlier.

What does it cost?

The app is free, and letters that open right away are free to seal. One payment of $29.99 in the app unlocks more ways to seal, for good. No subscription. Kits are $49 or $119, plus $7.95 shipping per order.

Shipping and returns

Kits ship to US addresses within 3 business days. Unopened kits can come back within 30 days for a refund. If a key arrives broken, send a photo and we send a new one free. Email support@sealmessenger.com first.
