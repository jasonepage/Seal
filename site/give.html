Before you give it
Ask first
Seal works best when they want to write. It does not let you see their phone or read what they write. Holding a key does not let you read anything either.
Which kit fits
Look at the end of their charging cable. A small oval plug is USB-C, for iPhone 15 or newer. A thin flat plug is Lightning, for iPhone 14 or earlier. The Lightning key has both plugs, so it fits every iPhone.
What it costs
The Seal app is free, and letters that open right away are free to seal. One payment of $29.99 in the app unlocks key holders, for good. No subscription. Kits are $49 for USB-C and $119 for Lightning, plus $7.95 shipping per order, however many kits are in it.
Shipping
US addresses only, including US territories and military addresses. Orders ship within 3 business days and usually arrive 3 to 8 business days after you order.
Returns
Unopened kits can come back within 30 days for a refund of the kit price. If a key arrives damaged or does not work, send us a photo within 30 days and we send a new one free. Email support@sealmessenger.com first.
Phones
Writing letters needs an iPhone with iOS 18 or newer. The people holding keys do not need an iPhone: a key opens their letters on any phone or computer at sealmessenger.com/open.
Privacy
Stripe handles payment. Your order details are used only to ship it and help you. Keys are made by Yubico, and key prices can change.
