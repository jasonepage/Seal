What if they lose the key?

You can replace a lost key before the letter opens. Keep a spare if the letter matters.

Can Seal read my letter?

No. Seal cannot read what you write.

Do they need an iPhone?

No. Opening works on any phone or computer.

What does it cost?

The app is free. Letters that open right away are free. One payment of $29.99 in the app unlocks more ways to seal. Kits are sold separately.

Shipping and returns

Kits ship to US addresses for $7.95. Unopened kits can be returned within 30 days. Email support@sealmessenger.com before sending one back.
