# App Store text for Seal 1.3

Seal is secret letters, opened on your terms. A message in a bottle you hand to one person.
Not a "digital legacy vault." Death is one use case, listed last, not the pitch.

Paste each block into App Store Connect. Character counts are checked by script.


## Category - App Information

Primary: Lifestyle. Secondary: Productivity.
Secret letters and messages in a bottle are a personal, everyday thing, not a work tool.
Do not pick Social Networking: Apple then expects chat moderation, blocking and reporting.

## Description

```
Write a secret letter. Seal it. Choose when it opens.

Seal is a message in a bottle you hand to one person. Put in your words, photos, your voice, a video, and the things you never wrote down anywhere else. Then seal it. Nobody can peek. Not Apple. Not us. Only the person you wrote it for can open it, with their own key or their iPhone, when your rule says it may.

THREE WAYS A LETTER OPENS
- Right away: like a message in a bottle. They can open it as soon as you seal it. Free.
- Break glass: for an emergency. It opens the moment enough people you trust plug in their keys together.
- When I go quiet: it opens only if you stop checking in for as long as you choose, from 7 days to a year. Seal warns you every day first, and one check-in stops it all.

WHAT PEOPLE SEAL
- A letter for a son's or daughter's 18th birthday
- A love letter, a confession, or an apology
- A surprise, a riddle, or a gift card code
- Passwords and account details for an emergency
- The words you want your family to have someday

SIMPLE FOR ANYONE
- Sign in with Face ID. No password to remember.
- Harbor, the Seal guide, shows you one next step at a time.
- Record your voice instead of typing. Opened letters can be read aloud.
- Check in with one tap on a reminder, or tell Siri "Check in with Seal."
- The person you write to does not need an iPhone. With the key you give them, they can open your letter at sealmessenger.com/open on any phone or computer.

A REAL KEY FOR REAL SECRETS
Give someone a small security key and the letter is theirs alone. Get a ready-made Seal key kit at sealmessenger.com/give.

NOBODY CAN PEEK
Everything is locked on your phone before it leaves it. Nothing we run can read, open or change your letters. Only locked data is stored, on Apple's iCloud servers. The code is public, so anyone can check how it works. No ads and no tracking.

PRICE
Free to download. Right away letters are free to seal. One payment of $29.99 unlocks Break glass and When I go quiet for good. No subscription. The people you write to never pay.

Opened on your terms.
```
