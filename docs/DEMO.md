# The demo video: shot list and cheat sheet

**Date:** 2026-09-16 · For the first public video of Seal, Reddit and Hacker News · Print this and tape it to the wall behind the camera.

## 0. The one rule

Every shot answers one of two questions: "what does this feel like at the
kitchen table" or "how do I know it is real". If a shot answers neither,
cut it. Two and a half minutes, three at the most. No slides, no music
under the talking, no logo until the end.

## 1. Before you press record (the day before)

- A build that runs against Production, on every phone in the video.
  Sign out and back in on each one so nothing from Development is in the
  keychain (GOTCHAS, Environments).
- The product live in App Store Connect and the Paid Apps agreement
  signed, so the price screen shows a real price and the seal goes
  through. If it is not, do not film the paywall; say "one purchase" and
  show nothing.
- Four YubiKeys: the owner's, the owner's backup, one per key holder.
  Two key holders on camera is the right number: one is not "it takes
  two", three is clutter.
- Three phones: the owner (you), key holder one (Mom), key holder two
  (Dad or Karen). The recipient can be one of the key holders; that is
  the common case and it saves a phone.
- Fake secrets. A made-up password, a made-up safe combination, a
  made-up policy number. Never a real one on camera, even blurred.
- Real words in the letter. Three lines you would actually say. That is
  the part people remember, and it is the part that cannot be faked.
- One printed key holder page, in a real drawer, with a real key on it.
- Charge everything. Turn on Do Not Disturb on every phone. Turn off
  Bigger text unless the person on camera needs it (then leave it on;
  it is honest).
- Ask Mom and whoever else is on camera, the day before, whether they
  are all right being filmed and whether they are all right with the
  subject. Say it out loud: "This is about what happens after I die.
  Are you okay filming that?" Their answer is the answer.

## 2. Camera and sound

- Two setups only. A: you and the phone, chest up, the phone held at
  chest height angled to camera so the screen reads. B: the table, from
  above at about forty five degrees, both phones and the keys in frame.
- Screen recordings from the phone itself for every app moment, cut in
  over the A shot. Turn on the tap indicator (Settings, Accessibility,
  Touch, AssistiveTouch is not it; use the built in screen recording and
  keep your thumb visible in the B shot instead). A finger on screen
  reads better than a dot.
- Lav mic on you. A second mic on the table for the key taps: the tap
  is a sound, and the brass mark pressing in should have a sound under
  it. Record room tone for ten seconds.
- Light from a window, phone screens at about 70 percent so they do not
  blow out. Kitchen light on. No ring light on the phone glass.
- The macro shot: the key touching the top of the phone, slow, once.
  Shoot it three times. It is your thumbnail.
- Keep the camera still. No handheld walking. Cuts, not moves.

## 3. The shots, in order, with what you say

Time stamps are targets. Say the lines your own way; keep the facts.

**00:00 Cold open, shot A, no title.** You hold up one key.
"This is a house key for a letter. When I go quiet for long enough, two
of these open it. Not me. Not Apple. Not the company that made the app.
Let me show you."

**00:10 Writing, screen recording.** Open Seal, Write, pick Mom. Type
three real lines. Add one fake secret and say it is fake. Add one photo.
Add two "what to do first" steps.
"Only her phone can ever read this. Not the app maker. I am going to
show you why in a minute."

**00:40 The handover, shot B.** Mom next to you. People, scan each
other's seal. Make her a key holder. She taps her key on your phone.
The brass mark presses in. Hand her the key. Put the printed page in
the drawer with it.
"She holds a key now. She cannot open anything with it. It takes two."
Then Dad, same thing, faster.

**01:05 Seal, screen recording.** Tap Seal. Pay (or cut around it).
"That is the one thing this app charges for. Once."

**01:15 Time travel, screen recording, and say what it is.** Open the
orange clock (the debug screen). On screen, say it plainly:
"The rule is 90 days of silence, 21 days of warnings, 14 more quiet
days. I am not going to make you wait. This is a test screen that moves
the clock. It is only in the test build."
Move to day 100. Show Mom's phone: "Jason has been quiet past the
limit." Mom starts the claim. Show your phone: the warning.
"Day 100. Mom says she thinks I am gone. My phone warns me every day
for three weeks. And if I am alive," tap, "I tap once, and everything
stops. No key needed. That is the whole point of the warnings: a long
hospital stay looks the same as death from the outside."

**01:50 The opening, shot B and screen recordings.** Time travel again,
past everything. Mom's phone: "Keys can be tapped now." She taps. Dad
taps. Mom's phone opens the envelope: the letter, the photo, the steps.
Then stop talking. Fifteen seconds of her reading it. Do not cut away
early. If she reacts, that is the video. If she does not, it is still
the video.

**02:15 The proof, laptop, shot A.** Open the capsule file. Run the
verifier script. It has no Seal code in it.
"Every signature and every timestamp, checked by a script you can read.
If we go out of business tomorrow, this still opens."
Then five seconds of the onboarding's curve figure.
"One key is one point on a line. One point could be any line. That is
not a policy we wrote. It is arithmetic."

**02:35 Close, shot A.** Hold up the key again.
"One purchase. No subscription. No account with us. No server that
holds your letters. Sealed envelopes for the people you leave behind."
Title card: Seal, the price, the link. Nothing else.

## 4. Say these, somewhere in the video or the post

- "Nobody can open one early. Not Apple, not us." Say it twice.
- "Seal does not know I died. It knows I went quiet. The warnings are
  for the hospital case."
- "There is no email reset, on purpose. That is why there is a backup
  key." (Show the backup key once.)
- "Key holders are met in person. That is how their key gets pinned."
- "A key holder learns nothing. Not the titles, not who the envelopes
  are for, not how many."
- "The record outlives us. Here is the file. Here is the script."
- "Face ID works for everything. A hardware key is recommended, never
  required."

## 5. Never say these

- "Will." Say "opens", "stops", "does". Every line in the app avoids
  it, and so should you.
- "When you die." Say "after a long silence" or "when you are gone" as
  the purpose, paired with the silence.
- "Unhackable", "military grade", "bank level", "zero knowledge". Say
  what it does; let them say the adjectives.
- "Custodian". It is "key holder".
- "Digital will" or anything with "will" as the legal document. Say
  "the papers a court makes public".
- Anything about a feature that is not built. No "coming soon".

## 6. Honesty rules for the shoot

- The time travel screen is shown and named on camera as a test screen.
  Never cut it so the countdown looks real time.
- Nobody dies in this video. It is "I went quiet". If Mom jokes about it,
  keep the joke; do not stage grief.
- Real reactions only. If you re-shoot the opening, tell the viewer it is
  the second take, or use the first take.
- Fake secrets, labelled as fake on screen or in the voice.
- The demo account is a test account. If any real identity or real
  person's data is on screen, stop and reshoot.

## 7. The post that goes with the video

**Hacker News title:** Show HN: Seal, sealed letters and passwords for
your family that open after a long silence and two hardware key taps

**Reddit title (r/privacy, r/yubikey, r/apple):** I built an app so my
mom gets my passwords and a letter when I'm gone, with two YubiKeys and
no company in the middle. Two minute demo.

**The body, in this order:** what it is in three sentences; the video;
"what it does not do" as a heading (cannot know you died, recipients
must be met in person, no email reset, what a key holder can see); how
the keys work in one paragraph with a link to docs/SDS.md; the verifier
script; the price and the words "no subscription"; what is not built
yet (the guided tour, the interview). Then stop.

**Your first comment, posted within a minute:** the link to the verifier
script and the docs folder, and one sentence: "Ask me anything about the
threat model. The honest limits are in PRODUCT.md section 8."

## 8. Cutting

- Cut on the taps. A key tap is a beat; land the edit on it.
- Every screen recording gets two seconds of the finger arriving before
  the tap. Viewers need to see where you are going.
- No speed ramps on the app. If it is slow, it is slow; cut instead.
- Captions on. Half the people who matter watch with the sound off, and
  half the people this is for are over sixty.
- Export at 4K, 30 frames per second, and a 1080 by 1920 vertical cut of
  the same edit for the phone crowd, with the B shot cropped tight on the
  keys.
